OBSERVED DATA CONTEXT

What this page does not claim

Observed quiz attempts and resource completion describe this local demonstration database. The offline dataset is synthetic and intentionally labeled as such. Neither source is evidence of causal learning improvement. A future learner study should use consent, a pre-test, post-test, delayed retention test, and a pre-registered comparison condition.
